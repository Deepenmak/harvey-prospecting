# Product Knowledge: Forward Deployment Engineering (FDE) with the PRISM Framework

## What We Sell
Optywise is a Forward Deployment Engineering firm that embeds senior AI/ML engineer pods inside client teams to take AI pilots to production in six weeks. It builds multi-agent systems, voice and chat agents, MCP integrations, RAG and model engineering on the client's own cloud and data. It serves enterprises and high-growth companies in the US and Canada, with a focus on insurance, healthcare RCM and fintech.

## Core Value Proposition
Senior AI engineers embed in your team and ship a working AI system into production on your infrastructure in six weeks, instead of leaving you with a deck or a stalled pilot.

## Key Benefits
- AI pilot to production in 6 weeks with one embedded senior pod
- You own all code, infrastructure, documentation and observability, so there is no vendor lock-in and capability is transferred rather than dependency created
- Senior AI/ML engineers only, employed and managed by Optywise (not contractors or juniors)
- Passes security and compliance review, with evals, guardrails and observability built in and work aligned toward HIPAA, SOC 2, NAIC and GDPR
- Deployed in your environment on your cloud (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud) and wired into legacy systems and messy real data

## Features
- PRISM framework: Probe, Right-size, Integrate, Secure, Mobilise
- Multi-Agent Systems (planning, retrieval, tool use, handoffs, multimodal)
- Voice & Chat Agents (24/7, multilingual, lead qualification, booking)
- MCP Engineering (native MCP servers and API-to-MCP conversion)
- RAG & Knowledge Grounding (cited, current answers over enterprise document stores)
- Model Engineering (selection, distillation, quantisation, on-prem and air-gapped deployment)
- Intelligent Automation, LLM Integration and Digital Transformation with AI
- Four-pillar AI security audit: code audit, infra audit, AI-as-subject defense (prompt injection, PII masking gateway) and continuous compliance

## Ideal Buyer Persona
A technical or operations leader at a mid-market or enterprise insurer, healthcare RCM organization, or fintech who has a mandate to ship AI but whose pilot has stalled before production. They are held accountable for cycle time, cost and quality in a real workflow (claims triage, underwriting submission intake, prior-auth, denials, reconciliation, AML ops). They are held back by legacy systems (policy admin, EHR/RCM, core banking), messy data, security and compliance review, and a backlog and engineering team with no capacity or senior AI/ML depth. They want a senior embedded team that ships a governed, auditable system on their own cloud within a quarter, and they want to own the code afterward. Success is a live system that real users depend on, with measurable gains such as claims or intake going from hours to minutes, plus compliance sign-off and a repeatable path to the next workflow. A secondary buyer is an AI platform or startup founder or CTO who needs forward-deployed engineering capacity to get their agents live inside their own customers' environments.

## Buying Triggers
When these events happen, prospects are most likely to buy:
- A regulatory deadline forcing the timeline, such as the CMS-0057-F prior-authorization rule or new state or NAIC AI requirements
- An AI pilot or proof of concept that has stalled, or a board or executive mandate to get AI into production this quarter
- Rising claims, intake or back-office volume and cost with no budget to add headcount, or an AI platform vendor needing to deploy agents inside a customer's environment

## Disqualifiers
Do NOT pursue prospects who:

- Small businesses or teams with no budget for a $60-150K fixed-scope engagement, or no decision-maker able to move fast and grant environment access
- Organizations outside the US and Canada, or those requiring only junior or low-cost offshore development resources
